# 🌿 Similipal Core Area — Phenology & Climate Analysis (2001–2024)

## 📌 Overall Understanding

This study analyzes **seasonal vegetation dynamics (NDVI, EVI, NDMI)** and their relationship with **climate variables (rainfall, temperature, ET, GPP)** across **Wet and Dry zones** in Similipal core area.

### Key themes explored:
- Seasonal vegetation growth cycles  
- Inter-annual variability  
- Climate–vegetation coupling  
- Long-term phenological trends (SOS, EOS, LOS)  
- Productivity changes over time  

---

## 1. Long-term Mean Seasonal NDVI Profile

### 🔍 What it shows:
- Mean NDVI (2001–2024) across seasons  
- Shaded region = variability (±1 SD)

### 📈 Observations:
- Clear **seasonal cycle**:
  - 🌱 Low NDVI → March–April (~0.64–0.66)
  - 🌿 Increase → May–September
  - 🌳 Peak → October–November (~0.85–0.88)
  - 🍂 Decline → December–February

- Dry zone slightly higher NDVI during peak  
- Others zone remains intermediate  

- Variability:
  - High during Aug–Sep → monsoon variability  

### 🧠 Insight:
Vegetation growth is strongly **monsoon-driven** with a lagged peak.

# 📊 Plot-wise Analysis (Part 4 — Final)

---

## 31. Evapotranspiration Stress (ET/PET)

### 🔍 What it shows:
- Ratio **ET / PET**
- 1 → no stress  
- 0 → maximum stress

### 📈 Observations:

#### Seasonal Pattern:
- **Mar–Apr** → lowest values (~0.15–0.4) → **high stress**
- **Jun–Oct** → peak (~0.7–0.8) → **low stress**
- **Nov–Feb** → gradual decline

#### Zone Comparison:
- Wet zone slightly higher than dry (less stress overall)
- Dry zone shows **stronger early-season stress**

### 🧠 Insight:
- Clear **monsoon-driven relief of stress**
- Pre-monsoon = **critical stress period**
- Dry zone ecosystems are more **water-limited**

---

## 32. Net Photosynthesis (PsnNet)

### 🔍 What it shows:
- Net carbon gain/loss
- Positive → carbon uptake  
- Negative → carbon loss

### 📈 Observations:

- **Mar–May** → strongly negative (~−20 to −25)
- **June** → transition to positive
- **Oct–Dec** → peak (~35–40)

#### Zone Comparison:
- Wet zone:
  - Earlier recovery (June)
- Dry zone:
  - Slight delay
  - Higher peak (~Dec)

### 🧠 Insight:
- Strong **seasonal carbon cycle**
- Pre-monsoon = **carbon loss phase**
- Post-monsoon = **maximum productivity**

---

## 33. Vegetation Moisture Indices (NDWI & NDMI)

### 📈 Observations:

#### NDWI:
- Both zones → **declining trend**
- Wet: ~−0.68 → ~−0.72  
- Dry: ~−0.70 → ~−0.74

👉 Indicates **decreasing surface moisture**

#### NDMI:
- Both zones → **increasing trend**
- Wet: ~0.25 → ~0.29+
- Dry: ~0.24 → ~0.27+

👉 Indicates **increasing canopy moisture**

### 🧠 Insight:
- Contrasting signals:
  - Surface moisture ↓  
  - Vegetation moisture ↑  
- Suggests:
  - Vegetation adaptation / deeper rooting
  - Improved water-use efficiency

---

## 34. Diurnal Temperature Range (DTR)

### 📈 Observations:

- ~10.5°C → ~8°C over period

#### Both zones:
- Similar behavior
- Sharp drops in recent years (2022–2024)

### 🧠 Insight:
- Lower DTR →

  - Increased moisture/cloud cover

👉 Indicates **climate shift towards humid/covered conditions**

---

## 36. NDVI vs ET & PET (Seasonal Co-variation)

### 📈 Observations:

#### Seasonal Dynamics:
- NDVI:
  - Rises from Apr → peaks Oct
- ET:
  - Follows NDVI closely
- PET:
  - Peaks earlier (Apr–May)

#### Key Relationships:
- Pre-monsoon:
  - PET high, ET low → **water limitation**
- Monsoon:
  - ET increases → matches NDVI growth
- Post-monsoon:
  - NDVI declines with ET

#### Zone Differences:
- Dry zone:
  - Stronger coupling NDVI–ET
- Wet zone:
  - Slight lag and smoother response

### 🧠 Insight:
- Vegetation growth is:
  - **Water-limited before monsoon**
  - **Energy-balanced during monsoon**

# ✅ FINAL SYNTHESIS (All Plots 1–36)

## 🌱 Phenology
- SOS: stable (wet), earlier shift (dry)
- EOS: stable
- LOS: temperature-sensitive (wet)

## 🌧 Climate Controls
- Weak rainfall control overall
- Strong **temperature & water stress effects**
- Pre-monsoon = limiting phase

## 🌿 Vegetation Dynamics
- Peak NDVI → stable
- NDVI amplitude → decreasing
- Integrated NDVI → **increasing strongly**

## 💧 Water & Stress
- ET/PET shows:
  - Strong seasonal stress cycle
- NDWI ↓ but NDMI ↑:
  - Surface drying but canopy adapting

## 🔥 Climate Signals
- DTR decreasing → more humid/cloudy conditions
- Warming impacts:
  - Shorter seasons (wet zone)
  - Earlier phenology (dry zone)

## 🌍 Productivity
- GPP → weak trend
- Integrated NDVI → strong increase

👉 Key conclusion:
> **Ecosystem is becoming greener and more productive overall,  
but under changing climate controls (temperature, moisture redistribution, and stress timing).**
